QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Do I need a smartwatch?

No. The Panic button works on a supported phone. Fall detection and wearable health readings require a compatible device.

Can MySentry get emergency help?

On plans with professional monitoring, the team can review an alert and contact emergency services when appropriate. Actual dispatch and arrival depend on the situation and service conditions.

What does Automated Call do?

It makes a scheduled pretend incoming call, so you have a natural reason to leave an uncomfortable moment. It is not an emergency call.

Can I use MySentry with Ring?

MySentry is available through the Ring App Store. See the Ring integration page for current options and compatibility.
