## Outreach Target Categories

### Category 1: Lone Worker Safety Resources
- **Sites**: SafetyLine Lone Worker, StaySafe, EHS Today, Safety+Health Magazine
- **Angle**: Contribute guest posts on lone worker technology trends, offer checklist as resource
- **Landing pages**: /employers, /use-cases/lone-worker-safety-app

### Category 2: Senior Care Resources
- **Sites**: AgingCare.com, A Place for Mom, SeniorLiving.org, AARP
- **Angle**: Provide fall prevention content, medical alert comparisons, caregiver tools
- **Landing pages**: /seniors, /use-cases/medical-alert-app-for-seniors, /features/fall-detection-app

### Category 3: HR and Compliance Publications
- **Sites**: SHRM, HR Dive, Compliance Week, HR Morning
- **Angle**: Duty of care for field workers, workplace safety technology ROI
- **Landing pages**: /employers, /use-cases/lone-worker-safety-app

### Category 4: Safety Technology Directories
- **Sites**: G2, Capterra, GetApp, Product Hunt, AlternativeTo
- **Angle**: Product listings with detailed feature descriptions and user reviews
- **Landing pages**: /features, /pricing, /compare

### Category 5: Women's Safety Organizations
- **Sites**: RAINN, National Network to End Domestic Violence, campus safety offices
- **Angle**: Provide safety resources, sponsor awareness campaigns, contribute expert content
- **Landing pages**: /females, /use-cases/safety-app-for-women

### Category 6: Industry-Specific Publications
- **Sites**: Real estate (Inman, RealTrends), healthcare (Home Health Care News), construction (Construction Dive)
- **Angle**: Industry-specific safety solutions and case studies
- **Landing pages**: /industries/* pages

## Outreach Process

1. **Research**: Identify 5-10 specific contacts per target category
2. **Personalize**: Reference their recent content and explain how your resource adds value
3. **Offer value first**: Share the linkable asset before asking for a link
4. **Follow up**: One follow-up email after 5-7 days
5. **Track**: Monitor backlinks via Google Search Console and Ahrefs/Semrush

## Priority Order

1. Safety technology directories (G2, Capterra) — highest ROI, easiest to execute
2. Senior care resources — large audience, high intent
3. HR and compliance publications — employer ICP
4. Women's safety organizations — consumer ICP
5. Industry-specific publications — niche authority
6. Lone worker safety resources — employer ICP

## Success Metrics

- **Target**: 20-30 referring domains within 6 months
- **Quality threshold**: DA 30+ sites only
- **Anchor text mix**: 60% branded, 25% descriptive, 15% exact match
